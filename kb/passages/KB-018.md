Excerpt (verbatim; “…” marks omitted text): “Cover bidding: Cover bidding is designed to give the appearance of genuine competition by way of supporting bids for the leading bid- rigger.” … “Bid rotation: In bid-rotation schemes, conspiring firms continue to bid but they agree to take turns being the winning (i.e., lowest qualifying) bidder in a group of tenders of a similar nature.”
