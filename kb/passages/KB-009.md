Excerpt (verbatim; “…” marks omitted text): “Every authority delegated with the financial powers of procuring goods in public interest shall have the responsibility and accountability to bring efficiency, economy, and transparency in matters relating to public procurement and for fair and equitable treatment of suppliers and promotion of competition in public procurement.” … “(v) Offers should be invited following a fair, transparent and reasonable procedure.”
